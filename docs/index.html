Field manual — wiring, placement & combat

01 / Connect. In Wire mode, click a card’s OUT port, then an IN port on another card. Each input accepts one wire; outputs can branch. Remove a connection below the board. Wires may cross and do not consume cells.

02 / Fit. In Move mode, click a component then its new top-left cell. Components cannot overlap or enter cutaway corners. Remove returns a component to the tray and disconnects its wires. Logic and hardware use the same cells.

03 / Pilot. W/S drive forward/reverse; A/D turn; Space fires with the starter wiring. Hold keys on the bench to see live pulses. Click outside buttons if needed. Your cannon fires in the robot’s facing direction. Purple shots are hostile.

04 / Grow. Win to choose one salvage card before the next bout. A clock can automate an output; an OR relay combines two inputs; armor costs room and adds health. Three bouts complete a run. A loss lets you edit and retry. Escape pauses; switching tabs pauses combat.
